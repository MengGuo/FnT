\chapter{\rev{Synthesis and Coordination under Global Temporal Logic Tasks}}
\label{chap:top-down}

\rev{Chapter~\ref{chap:bottom-up} takes prescribed local temporal tasks as
inputs and coordinates their execution. Here the input is a temporal
specification for the whole team. The planning problem is to derive subtasks,
their temporal dependencies, and feasible robot assignments while preserving
the team-level specification. The construction must also remain tractable
for heterogeneous teams and large workspaces.}
\rev{The resulting framework combines global-task synthesis with
multi-robot coordination.}
Section~\ref{ch04:sec:po_decomp_assignment} converts accepted automaton
behaviors into subtask occurrences and compact temporal-dependence structures
for assignment. Section~\ref{ch04:subsec:task} extends the construction to
large fleets through hierarchical abstraction. Section~\ref{ch04:sec:online_continual_tasks}
then addresses continual operation, where new demands require online revision
without discarding the executed prefix or the global temporal structure.
